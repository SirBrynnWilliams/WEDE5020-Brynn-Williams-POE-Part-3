A Step at a Time

How the Adoption Journey Works

Every child and family situation is different. These are the general stages in South Africa’s national adoption guidance; the responsible adoption agency can explain what applies to your circumstances (South African Government, [s.a.]).

  1. Start with an adoption agency

    Ask an adoption agency about its role, current authorisation, process, and how to begin.

  2. Screening and home study

    The agency assesses prospective adoptive parents and prepares a home study.

  3. Registration and matching

    If the assessment is favourable, the agency may register prospective parents while it considers children who are available for adoption.

  4. Meet the child

    If a child is available and a possible match is identified, the agency arranges a meeting and discusses the next decision with you.

  5. Children’s Court

    If you decide to proceed, the agency sends its report to the Children’s Court for the adoption decision and order. The Department of Social Development then receives the court report for its checks and records.

The process, eligibility, time, and costs depend on the case. See the South African Government’s national adoption guidance and confirm current details with the responsible agency (South African Government, [s.a.]).

Clear answers build trust

What to Check Before You Begin

The South African Government ([s.a.]) advises prospective adoptive parents to approach an adoption agency, which screens applicants and helps identify a child available for adoption. Ask which organisation and registered professionals are responsible for the legal process in your case. This website does not publish unverified certificates or adoption totals.

Confirm who handles the adoption

Ask which registered adoption agency or adoption social worker will manage screening, matching, and the court report. Confirm their current role and credentials with the relevant authorities.

Request clear written information

Ask for the current authorisation details, fees, expected steps, privacy safeguards, and a contact for questions or complaints before making a decision.

Ask how outcomes are reported

For adoption numbers or programme studies, request a dated report that explains its time period and method. Use aggregate figures only and protect every child’s identity.

Read the official South African adoption guidance for the national process and contact the Department of Social Development to confirm current details (South African Government, [s.a.]).

Services We Provide

Adoption Process Breakdown

We provide a complete overview of the adoption process to help you acquire a child of your choice. Our team guides you through each step, from the initial application to the final placement, ensuring you feel supported at all times.

Legal Standards and Requirements

We ensure that all adoptions meet the strict legal requirements for child safety in South Africa. Our staff assists couples in understanding the legal documentation needed to provide a secure future for their new family member.

Counselling and Guidance Sessions

Professional counselling is available for new couples who are adopting children. We also provide specialized children guidance sessions on weekends during selected time intervals to help with emotional transitions and family bonding.

Adoption Centre Volunteering

Beyond our adoption services, we welcome community members to get involved through our volunteering program. This allows the local community to support our children in Walmer and assist in creating a learning-rich environment.

Jahs Adoption Centre

Founded in 2018 in Gqeberha, Jahs Adoption Centre is a non-profit organisation dedicated to providing safe housing and finding loving forever homes for vulnerable children.
