Welcome to Jahs Adoption Centre

Jahs Adoption Centre is a non-profit organization based in Gqeberha, dedicated to bridging the gap between vulnerable children and forever families. We provide a safe haven where children can eat, sleep and learn in a rebuilt modern Facility

Bridging the Gap Between Children and Forever Families

Our Core Activities

  • Adoption Facilitation: Guiding parents though the journey of finding a new family.
  • Safe Housing: Providing an improved living quarters in our Walmer facility
  • Educational Support: Ensuring children grow up in an learning rich environment.
  • Community Outreach: Responding to abandonment across Nelson Mandela Bay.

Why Choose Us?

We prioritize child-centered care and family integrity. Our rebuilt facility in Walmer offers a safe and professional environment that ensures every child is prepared for their new home through specialized support and ethical screening processes.
